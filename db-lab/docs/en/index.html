Database Study Notes Interview prep · the shop practice database
中EN日

Using simulated data from "shop", a Taiwanese e-commerce store (customers, orders, order items, products and categories, about 300,000 rows), practice queries, data modeling and common interview concepts across nine databases: PostgreSQL, Redis, MongoDB, Cassandra, Neo4j, TimescaleDB, pgvector, Elasticsearch and InfluxDB.

CheatSheet PostgreSQL: SQL execution order, JOINs, NULL, window functions, transactions, JSONB / UPSERT, index types (partial, expression, GIN, GiST, BRIN). Redis: data structures, transactions and Lua, cache penetration / breakdown / avalanche, distributed locks, persistence, cluster. MongoDB: queries and the aggregation pipeline, embedding vs referencing, indexes and ESR, transactions and sharding. Cassandra: partition and clustering keys, query-first table design, tombstones, consistency levels, LWT. Neo4j: Cypher patterns, paths and shortest paths, MERGE, graph data modeling, PROFILE. TimescaleDB: hypertables and chunks, time_bucket, gapfill, continuous aggregates, compression, retention. pgvector: distance operators, semantic search and recommendations, HNSW vs IVFFlat, filtering and multi-tenancy, quantization, hybrid search. Elasticsearch: inverted index and analyzers, Query DSL, BM25 relevance, aggregations, nested, writes and near real-time, shards. InfluxDB: line protocol, tags vs fields and cardinality, InfluxQL, Flux, counters and rates, retention and downsampling. Question Bank PostgreSQL: 43 exercises, 10 traps, 11 lab steps; Redis: 26 exercises, 8 traps; MongoDB: 27 exercises, 8 traps, 6 lab steps; Cassandra: 28 exercises, 11 traps, 6 lab steps; Neo4j: 24 exercises, 12 traps, 5 lab steps; TimescaleDB: 18 exercises, 6 traps, 4 lab steps; pgvector: 20 exercises, 8 traps; InfluxDB: 24 exercises, 11 traps, 6 lab steps; Elasticsearch: 26 exercises, 11 traps, 6 lab steps. With hints, answers, explanations and the real output of each correct answer.
